Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. The page downloads two small AI models from Google's open-source MediaPipe project and runs them inside your browser. Your photo is never sent to a server, and nothing is saved after you close the tab.

How does a tap know what to lift?

A tap-guided model (MediaPipe Magic Touch) outlines the subject under your finger. A second model checks whether that subject is a person: if you tapped just a face or a shirt, it lifts the whole person, and it trims stray bits of background. Select all people finds everyone first and lifts each one the same way. If two people touch, they may lift together: switch to Remove and tap the one you don't want.
